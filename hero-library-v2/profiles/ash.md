# Ash — SSR Ranger DPS

**Rarity:** SSR  
**Faction:** Ranger  
**Role:** DPS  
**Position:** Backline  

## Character Overview
Ash is an SSR Ranger DPS hero specializing in Physical damage and monster hunting. She combines single-target and multi-target archery attacks with a passive ability that increases monster damage for the two highest-ATK Rangers in her squad. Her defining ability, Focus, makes her especially useful in Ranger squads built for PvE encounters.

## Skills
| Skill | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Precision Shot** | Auto Attack | Fires an arrow at a single enemy, dealing Physical damage. |
| **One Stone Two Birds** | Active | Launches two special arrows that attack two randomly selected targets, dealing Physical damage to each. |
| **Eagle Spirit Hunt** | Ultimate | Fires a giant arrow that deals substantial Physical damage to a single enemy. |
| **Focus** | Passive | Increases damage dealt to monsters by the two Rangers with the highest ATK in the team. |
| **Tenacity** | Support | Provides a fixed bonus to ATK, HP, and DEF. |

## Skill Upgrade Priority
1. **Focus** — Her defining PvE ability. Improves monster damage for the team's two highest-ATK Rangers.
2. **Eagle Spirit Hunt** — Powerful single-target damage for tougher enemies.
3. **One Stone Two Birds** — Provides additional damage against multiple targets.
4. **Precision Shot** — Basic single-target damage; lowest upgrade priority.

Tenacity is fixed and does not require normal skill upgrades.

## Gear Priority
**UR Sword → UR Gauntlet.** As a DPS hero, Ash benefits most from offensive equipment that improves her Physical damage output.

## Recommended Team Composition
- **Frontline:** Shadow + Louis
- **Backline:** Cynthia + Ash + Bell

This example Ranger lineup uses two tanks for protection, Bell for support, and Cynthia and Ash for damage.

**Best use:** Monster hunting and PvE content, where Ash's Focus passive can improve the performance of the squad's strongest Rangers.
