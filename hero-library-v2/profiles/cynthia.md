## Character overview
Ranger damage dealer who builds Moonlight stacks and combines offensive pressure with critical strikes.

## Skills
- **Moon Blade — Auto Attack:** Generates Moonlight stacks and scales with Ranger allies.
- **Lunar Hunt — Active Skill:** Attacks multiple targets and generates Moonlight stacks.
- **Moonfall Dance — Ultimate Skill:** Area damage and critical-strike pressure.
- **Moon's Blessing — Passive Skill:** Improves critical strike performance.
- **Tenacity — Fixed Support Skill:** Fixed combat-stat and cooldown bonuses; no upgrade priority.

## Recommended skill upgrade priority
1. **Moonfall Dance** — Main burst-damage ability.
2. **Lunar Hunt** — Multi-target damage and Moonlight stack generation.
3. **Moon's Blessing** — Critical-hit improvement.
4. **Moon Blade** — Basic attack damage and Moonlight generation.

**Tenacity** is fixed and does not require upgrading.

## Gear priority
**UR Sword → UR Gauntlet.** Prioritize damage for backline DPS.
