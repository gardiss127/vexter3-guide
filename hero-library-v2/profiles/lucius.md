## Character Overview
Lucius is a frontline Warrior tank who combines Physical damage with defensive utility against monsters. His strongest offensive skill delivers a powerful axe strike with a stun enhancement, while his passive reduces damage received from monsters. He is especially useful in PvE battles where monster damage reduction and crowd control are valuable.

## Skills
- **Battle Axe (Auto Attack):** Swings a great axe, dealing Physical damage to one enemy. Star upgrades increase damage.
- **Decapitation (Active):** Delivers a powerful two-handed axe strike against a single enemy, dealing substantial Physical damage.
- **Whirlwind Cut (Ultimate):** Attacks an enemy directly in front of Lucius with a heavy axe swing. Star upgrades increase damage and extend the stun duration.
- **God of War Blessing (Passive):** Reduces damage Lucius receives from monsters. Higher skill and star levels strengthen the defensive effect.
- **Tenacity (Support):** Provides a fixed bonus to ATK, HP, and DEF.

## Skill Upgrade Priority
1. **God of War Blessing** — Primary defensive passive for surviving monster encounters.
2. **Whirlwind Cut** — Damage and crowd-control utility through its stun enhancement.
3. **Decapitation** — Strong single-target Physical damage.
4. **Battle Axe** — Basic attack damage; lowest priority for limited upgrade resources.

**Tenacity** is fixed and does not require normal skill investment.

## Gear Priority
**UR Armor → UR Boots.** As a frontline tank, Lucius benefits most from defensive gear that improves his durability and keeps him alive to absorb incoming damage.

## Recommended Team Composition
- **Frontline:** Lucius + Arthur or Daskal (Warrior Tank)
- **Backline:** Marlena (Warrior DPS) + Zoya (Warrior DPS) + Harper (Warrior Support)

This is a faction-compatible example, not necessarily his strongest lineup. Lucius can fill a frontline position when preferred Warrior tanks are unavailable.
