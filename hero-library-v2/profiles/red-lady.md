## Character overview
Backline Ranger DPS specializing in Energy Damage, priority-target burst, increased damage taken debuffs, and a chance to stun dangerous enemy damage dealers.

## Skills
- **Blood Blade — Auto Attack:** Throws a scalpel at a single enemy, dealing Energy Damage. Star upgrades strengthen the attack.
- **Bloodthirsty Slaughter — Active Skill:** Focuses an enemy with a powerful Energy attack and temporarily increases damage the target takes.
- **Crimson Rose — Ultimate Skill:** Strikes the two enemies with the highest ATK and can stun them. Star upgrades increase damage and allow an additional target.
- **Lone Flower — Passive Skill:** Increases Red Lady's ATK in combat.
- **Tenacity — Fixed Support Skill:** Fixed ATK, HP, DEF and cooldown speed bonuses; no upgrade priority.

## Recommended skill upgrade priority
1. **Crimson Rose** — Priority-target burst and stun potential.
2. **Bloodthirsty Slaughter** — Strong attack and vulnerability debuff.
3. **Lone Flower** — Overall ATK increase.
4. **Blood Blade** — Basic attack damage.
