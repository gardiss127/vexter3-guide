# Grenwald — SSR Warlock DPS

**Rarity:** SSR
**Faction:** Warlock
**Role:** DPS
**Position:** Backline
**Damage Type:** Energy
**Exclusive Weapon:** None
**Awakening:** Not currently available

## Character Overview
Grenwald is a Warlock damage dealer specializing in Energy damage, area-of-effect attacks, and increased damage against monsters. Soul Capture Spell damages multiple enemies and provides a temporary ATK boost through star upgrades. His passive improves his effectiveness against monsters.

## Skills
- **Cast Spell (Auto Attack):** Unleashes lightning from his wand, dealing Energy damage to a single enemy.
- **Heartbreaker Spell (Active):** Summons a soul claw that strikes a single enemy for substantial Energy damage. Star upgrades increase its damage.
- **Soul Capture Spell (Ultimate):** Draws spirits from enemies within range, dealing Energy damage to multiple targets. Star upgrades increase damage and grant a temporary ATK boost.
- **Exorcism Spell (Passive):** Increases Grenwald's damage dealt to monsters. Star upgrades strengthen this bonus.
- **Tenacity (Support):** Provides a fixed bonus to ATK, HP, and DEF.

## Skill Upgrade Priority
1. **Soul Capture Spell** — Primary area-damage ability, with additional offensive benefits from star upgrades.
2. **Exorcism Spell** — Improves damage against monsters, especially valuable for PvE.
3. **Heartbreaker Spell** — Reliable single-target Energy damage.
4. **Cast Spell** — Basic attack damage; lowest upgrade priority.

Tenacity is fixed and does not require regular upgrades.

## Gear Priority
**UR Sword → UR Gauntlet.** As a DPS hero, Grenwald benefits most from offensive equipment.

## Recommended Team Composition
- **Frontline:** Hastar (Warlock Tank); second Warlock Tank.
- **Backline:** Grenwald (DPS); second Warlock DPS; Stellar (Support).

Grenwald pairs particularly well with Stellar in monster encounters. Her Wishing Star can strengthen Warlock damage dealers, while his Exorcism Spell provides additional monster damage.

## Important Gameplay Distinctions
- **Soul Capture Spell** deals area damage, not single-target damage.
- Its temporary ATK boost requires the relevant star upgrade.
- **Exorcism Spell** increases damage specifically against monsters.
