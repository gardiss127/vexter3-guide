# Shadow

**Faction:** Ranger
**Role:** Tank
**Position:** Frontline
**Damage type:** Physical

## Character overview
Shadow is a frontline Ranger Tank who contributes area attacks, enemy defense reduction, and damage mitigation, including protection against Energy damage.

## Skills
- **Smoke Cartridge — Auto Attack:** Throws three smoke bombs at a single enemy, dealing Physical damage.
- **Shadow Strike — Active Skill:** Damages enemies in an area and temporarily reduces their DEF.
- **Shadow Hunt — Ultimate Skill:** Attacks all enemies and temporarily reduces Energy damage received.
- **Shadow Veil — Passive Skill:** Reduces Energy damage taken by Shadow.
- **Tenacity — Fixed Support Skill:** Increases ATK, HP, DEF and skill cooldown speed; not upgraded.

## Recommended skill upgrade priority
1. **Shadow Hunt** — Broad damage and Energy damage mitigation.
2. **Shadow Strike** — Area damage and enemy DEF reduction.
3. **Shadow Veil** — Improves Shadow's survival against Energy damage.
4. **Smoke Cartridge** — Improves regular attack output.

**Tenacity** is fixed and does not require upgrading.

## Gear priority
**UR Armor → UR Boots.** Prioritize survivability for the frontline Tank.

## Recommended team composition
- **Frontline:** Shadow (Tank) + Louis (Tank)
- **Backline:** Cynthia (DPS) + Red Lady (DPS) + Bell (Support)

**Exclusive Weapon:** None.
