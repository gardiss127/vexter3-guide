# Billy — UR Warlock Tank

**Faction:** Warlock
**Rarity:** UR
**Role:** Tank
**Position:** Frontline
**Damage Type:** Physical Damage

## Character Overview

Billy is a defensive Warlock Tank who specializes in protecting high-attack allies from Energy Damage and stun effects. Unlike tanks that focus primarily on shielding themselves, Billy provides defensive benefits to teammates while reducing the damage he personally receives. His strongest qualities are Energy Damage protection, stun resistance, and overall survivability.

## Skills

### Wooden Frame — Auto Attack
Strikes a single enemy with a marionette, dealing Physical Damage. Star upgrades increase its damage.

### Fate's Thread — Active Skill
Damages one enemy while reducing Energy Damage received by the two highest-ATK allies. Star upgrades strengthen the reduction and eventually protect an additional ally.

### Truth and Lies — Ultimate Skill
Damages enemies within range and grants the three highest-ATK allies a chance to resist stuns. Star upgrades improve stun immunity. An additional enhancement temporarily reduces damage received by allied defense heroes.

### Puppet — Passive Skill
Reduces all damage Billy receives. Star upgrades improve his damage reduction and provide additional protection against Energy Damage.

### Tenacity — Support Skill
Fixed support skill that increases ATK, HP, DEF, and skill cooldown speed. Does not require upgrading.

## Recommended Skill Upgrade Priority

1. **Truth and Lies** — Prioritize team-wide stun protection, area damage, and the defensive enhancement for allied tanks.
2. **Fate's Thread** — Strengthens protection for highest-ATK heroes against Energy Damage.
3. **Puppet** — Improves Billy's personal survivability so he can continue protecting the team.
4. **Wooden Frame** — Additional basic attack damage is less important than his defensive abilities.

**Tenacity** is fixed and does not require upgrading.

## Gear Priority

**UR Armor → UR Boots**

Billy is a frontline tank, so defensive equipment takes priority over offensive gear.
