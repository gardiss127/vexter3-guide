## Character overview
Frontline Ranger tank with ally protection, damage mitigation, self-healing, and survival mechanics.

## Skills
- **Shackles — Auto Attack:** Single-target chain attack.
- **Force Link — Active Skill:** Reduces Energy damage taken by allied heroes with the highest DEF.
- **Cross Whip — Ultimate Skill:** Area attack that heals Louis and reduces damage received.
- **Indomitable Spirit — Passive Skill:** Damage reduction and a one-time survival effect against fatal damage.
- **Tenacity — Fixed Support Skill:** Fixed combat bonuses, not upgraded.

## Recommended skill upgrade priority
1. **Cross Whip** — Primary combination of area damage, self-healing and damage reduction.
2. **Indomitable Spirit** — Improves survivability.
3. **Force Link** — Protects allied heroes.
4. **Shackles** — Basic attack damage.

**Tenacity** is fixed and does not require upgrading.

## Gear priority
**UR Armor → UR Boots** to strengthen frontline survival.

## Recommended team composition
**Frontline:** Shadow + Louis.
