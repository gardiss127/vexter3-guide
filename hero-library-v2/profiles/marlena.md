## Character overview
Marlena is a powerful Energy DPS hero who specializes in explosive attacks, critical hits, and stacking *Battle Will*. Her damage potential and survivability improve as she accumulates stacks, making her especially effective in Warrior-heavy teams. She also weakens enemies through Dread.

## Skills
- **Skybreaker Slash:** Launches a sword wind at a single enemy, dealing Energy damage. Critical hits generate Battle Will stacks, increasing her damage dealt and reducing damage received. Her stack capacity scales with the number of allied Warriors.
- **Crimson Bloom:** Launches an explosive blossom that damages all enemies. With sufficient Battle Will stacks, it applies Dread, reducing enemy damage output.
- **Peerless Blade:** Releases multiple sword winds against randomly selected enemies, dealing heavy Energy damage. Star upgrades increase the number and strength of the attacks.
- **War God's Will:** Increases Energy damage and launches an opening attack against the enemies with the highest HP, immediately generating Battle Will stacks.
- **Tenacity:** Increases ATK, HP, and DEF while improving skill cooldown speed.

## Recommended skill upgrade priority
1. **Peerless Blade** — Her major burst-damage ability and a strong source of overall damage.
2. **War God's Will** — Boosts Energy damage and generates Battle Will immediately at the start of combat.
3. **Crimson Bloom** — Provides area damage and reduces enemy damage through Dread.
4. **Skybreaker Slash** — Contributes damage and generates Battle Will through critical hits.

*Tenacity is a fixed support skill and does not require upgrading.*

## Gear priority
**Sword → Gauntlet → other equipment.** Prioritize ATK, critical-hit effectiveness, and Energy damage bonuses where available to maximize her offensive potential.

## Recommended team composition
**Frontline:** Arthur — Tank; second Warrior Tank.
**Backline:** Marlena — DPS; Warrior DPS; Warrior Support.

A full Warrior team maximizes Marlena's Battle Will stack capacity and benefits from her faction-wide Awakening bonus.

## Awakening abilities
- **Level 1 — Skybreaker Slash Enhancement:** Critical hits generate Battle Will stacks. Stack capacity scales with the number of Warrior heroes, and each stack increases Marlena's damage dealt while reducing damage received.
- **Level 10 — Crimson Bloom Enhancement:** Crimson Bloom damages all enemies and applies Dread when Marlena has accumulated sufficient Battle Will, reducing enemy damage output.
- **Level 20 — Blade Tempest:** At the beginning of battle, attacks the enemies with the highest HP and immediately gains Battle Will stacks.
- **Level 30 — Soul of Warrior:** Increases ATK, HP, and DEF for all allied Warrior heroes during battle.
- **Level 40 — Peerless Blade Enhancement:** Unleashes additional sword winds at reduced individual damage, greatly increasing the number of attacks.

**Exclusive Weapon:** None.
