# Stellar — SSR Warlock Support

**Rarity:** SSR
**Faction:** Warlock
**Role:** Support
**Position:** Backline
**Damage Type:** Physical (offensive skills)
**Exclusive Weapon:** None
**Awakening:** Not currently available

## Character Overview
Stellar is a Warlock support hero who specializes in increasing damage against monsters, strengthening allied Warlock damage dealers, and providing emergency healing. Her strongest contribution comes from supporting high-damage Warlock teammates rather than dealing damage herself. She is particularly useful in monster-focused encounters, where her buffs can improve the squad's overall performance.

## Skills
- **Meteorite (Auto Attack):** Launches stardust at a single enemy, dealing Physical damage.
- **Destructive Comet (Active):** Summons a comet that damages enemies within range. Star upgrades also allow it to temporarily increase the damage monsters receive.
- **Wishing Star (Ultimate):** Summons a meteor shower that increases damage dealt to monsters by the top two Warlock allies. Star upgrades improve the buff and extend its duration.
- **Final Starlight (Passive):** When Stellar receives fatal damage, she heals the allied hero with the lowest HP before dying. Star upgrades increase the healing.
- **Tenacity (Support):** Provides a fixed bonus to ATK, HP, and DEF.

## Skill Upgrade Priority
1. **Wishing Star** — Primary support ability; strengthens Warlock allies against monsters.
2. **Destructive Comet** — Area damage and, with star investment, a monster damage-taken debuff.
3. **Final Starlight** — Emergency healing, activated only when Stellar takes fatal damage.
4. **Meteorite** — Basic single-target damage; lowest investment priority.

Tenacity is fixed and does not require regular upgrades.

## Gear Priority
**UR Armor → UR Boots.** Defensive equipment helps Stellar survive to maintain her buffs. Fatal-damage healing is a backup effect, not a reason to sacrifice survivability.

## Recommended Team Composition
- **Frontline:** Hastar (Tank); second Warlock Tank.
- **Backline:** Warlock DPS; Warlock DPS; Stellar (Support).

Stellar works best alongside strong Warlock damage dealers who benefit from Wishing Star, especially during monster encounters.

## Important Gameplay Distinctions
- **Final Starlight does not prevent Stellar's death.** It heals another ally when she takes fatal damage.
- Her monster-focused buffs should not be mistaken for universal PvP damage bonuses.
