## Character overview
Griffith is a defensive Ranger hero specializing in reducing damage taken from monsters. His abilities combine single-target attacks with protective effects that help nearby allies survive longer. His greatest strength is PvE survivability, making him useful for monster encounters where keeping the squad alive is a priority.

## Skills
- **Serpent Flute (Auto Attack):** Summons a spirit snake to attack a single enemy. The skill is labeled Energy DMG, although its description mentions Physical DMG.
- **Venomous Bite (Active):** Commands the spirit snake to bite a single enemy, dealing substantial Energy damage.
- **Serpent Veil (Ultimate):** Summons a protective spirit snake that circles Griffith, temporarily reducing monster damage taken by allied heroes within range. Star upgrades improve the protection and its duration.
- **Serpent Resonance (Passive):** Reduces damage Griffith takes from monsters during combat. Higher skill levels and star upgrades strengthen the effect.
- **Tenacity (Support):** Provides a fixed bonus to ATK, HP, and DEF. Unlocks at the required hero level and star rank.

## Skill upgrade priority
1. **Serpent Veil** — Protects nearby allies from monster damage, strengthening Griffith's main tanking contribution.
2. **Serpent Resonance** — Improves Griffith's personal durability against monsters.
3. **Venomous Bite** — Improves his single-target active damage after defensive skills are developed.
4. **Serpent Flute** — Basic attack improvement; lowest priority for his defensive role.

Tenacity is a fixed support skill and does not require normal skill upgrades.

## Gear priority
**UR Armor → UR Boots.** Prioritize defensive gear to improve Griffith's survivability as a frontline tank.

## Recommended team composition
- **Frontline:** Griffith (Tank), Shadow (Tank)
- **Backline:** Cynthia (DPS), Red Lady (DPS), Bell (Support)

This provides two frontline tanks, two damage dealers, and one support. Griffith is particularly suited to monster-focused encounters because of his defensive abilities.
