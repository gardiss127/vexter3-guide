## Character Overview

Bella is an SSR Warrior Tank who specializes in reducing incoming monster damage while contributing Physical Damage and weakening enemy attacks. Unlike tanks built primarily around shields or healing, Bella's defensive abilities focus heavily on monster encounters, making her particularly useful for PvE activities.

## Skills

- **Swing (Auto Attack):** Swings a giant axe at a single enemy, dealing Physical Damage. Star upgrades increase its damage.
- **Crimson Prayer (Active Skill):** Temporarily reduces damage taken from monsters. Upgrades strengthen the damage reduction effect.
- **Bloody Requiem (Ultimate Skill):** Attacks enemies within range using dual axes, dealing heavy Physical Damage and temporarily reducing their ATK.
- **Purity (Passive Skill):** Reduces damage taken from monsters throughout combat, improving Bella's survivability in PvE encounters.
- **Tenacity (Support Skill):** Provides fixed ATK, HP, and DEF bonuses. This SSR version does not include the cooldown speed bonus shown on UR heroes.

## Skill Upgrade Priority

1. **Purity** — Consistent passive monster-damage reduction throughout combat.
2. **Crimson Prayer** — Additional defensive protection against monster attacks.
3. **Bloody Requiem** — Area damage combined with an enemy ATK reduction.
4. **Swing** — Basic single-target damage, less important than Bella's defensive abilities.

Tenacity is fixed and does not require upgrading.

## Gear Priority

**Armor → Boots**

As a frontline Tank, Bella should prioritize defensive gear that improves her survivability. Use the highest-quality gear available, preferably UR.
