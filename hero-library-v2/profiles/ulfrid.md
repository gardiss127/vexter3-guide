## Character overview
Ulfrid is a frontline Warlock Tank who combines personal durability with teamwide protection. His Berserk state reflects incoming damage, while his Ultimate reduces damage taken by allied heroes. He is especially valuable for keeping a Warlock squad alive during prolonged battles.

## Skills
- **Sharp Claw — Auto Attack:** Swings his claws to deal Physical Damage to a single enemy. Star upgrades increase damage.
- **Lunar Howl — Active Skill:** Enters Berserk state, reflecting a portion of incoming damage back to attackers. Star upgrades improve reflection strength and duration. His Exclusive Weapon can also increase DEF during Berserk.
- **Throat Bite — Ultimate Skill:** Lunges at an enemy, dealing Physical Damage and temporarily reducing damage taken by all allied heroes. Star upgrades strengthen the teamwide protection.
- **Wolf Bloodline — Passive Skill:** Reduces damage Ulfrid receives during battle. Star upgrades and his Exclusive Weapon strengthen this defensive effect.
- **Tenacity — Fixed Support Skill:** Provides ATK, HP, DEF, and skill cooldown speed bonuses. Does not require upgrading.

## Recommended skill upgrade priority
1. **Throat Bite** — His most valuable protective ability because it reduces damage taken by the entire team.
2. **Wolf Bloodline** — Improves consistent damage mitigation and frontline survivability.
3. **Lunar Howl** — Reflects damage back to attackers, with additional defensive benefits from his Exclusive Weapon.
4. **Sharp Claw** — Basic attack damage is less important than defensive abilities.

**Tenacity** is fixed and does not require upgrading.

## Gear priority
**UR Armor → UR Boots.** Prioritize HP, DEF, and survivability to help Ulfrid withstand frontline damage and maintain his protective abilities.
