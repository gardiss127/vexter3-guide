## Character overview
Annie is a backline Warlock DPS specializing in Energy Damage, damage over time, and targeting dangerous enemies. Her abilities apply Burn, while Awakening introduces Scorch, a stacking damage-over-time effect that amplifies several of her attacks. Allied Warlock count influences her Scorch stack capacity, rewarding a full Warlock squad.

## Skills
- **Candy — Auto Attack:** Conjures a magical candy that deals Energy Damage to a single enemy. Star upgrades increase damage.
- **Surprise Gift — Active Skill:** Throws a gift box that damages enemies within range and applies Burn, dealing Energy Damage over time. Star upgrades extend Burn duration and increase damage.
- **Candy Jar — Ultimate Skill:** Summons explosive candy jars targeting the two enemies with the highest ATK, dealing Energy Damage and applying Burn. Star upgrades allow an additional target and add significant bonus damage against enemies already Burning.
- **Charged Gummies — Passive Skill:** Increases Annie's Energy Damage during battle. Star upgrades strengthen this bonus.
- **Tenacity — Fixed Support Skill:** Provides ATK, HP, DEF, and skill cooldown speed bonuses. Fixed at level 1 and does not require upgrading.

## Recommended skill upgrade priority
1. **Candy Jar** — Strongest burst ability, targeting high-ATK enemies and gaining bonus damage against Burning targets.
2. **Surprise Gift** — Reliable area damage and Burn application, establishing damage-over-time effects that other abilities exploit.
3. **Charged Gummies** — Improves overall Energy Damage.
4. **Candy** — Useful basic attack damage, but less important than Burn-focused abilities.

**Tenacity** is fixed and does not require upgrading.

## Gear priority
**UR Sword → UR Gauntlet.** Prioritize offensive equipment to increase Annie's Energy Damage and attack effectiveness.

## Awakening
**Available.** Five milestones confirmed from user-supplied screenshots:

- **Awaken Level 1 — Candy (Burn and Scorch):** Candy gains a chance to inflict Burn. Whenever the team successfully applies Burn, Annie adds a Scorch stack to the target. Scorch deals damage over time, with maximum stack count based on the number of allied Warlock heroes.
- **Awaken Level 10 — Surprise Gift (Scorch Synergy):** Increases Surprise Gift damage, with additional damage for each Scorch stack on the target.
- **Awaken Level 20 — Candy Jar (Scorch Detonation):** Deals additional Energy Damage to all enemies affected by Scorch, scaling with the number of stacks on each target.
- **Awaken Level 30 — Soul of Warlock (Faction Enhancement):** Increases ATK, HP, and DEF for all allied Warlock heroes during battle.
- **Awaken Level 40 — Sweet Troublemaker (Enhanced Burn and Scorch):** When Annie's normal attack triggers Burn, applies an additional Burn to a random enemy hero. Scorch lasts longer, and its maximum stack capacity increases beyond the allied-Warlock-based limit.
