# Bestar — SSR Ranger DPS

**Rarity:** SSR
**Faction:** Ranger
**Role:** DPS
**Position:** Backline
**Damage Type:** Physical
**Exclusive Weapon:** None
**Awakening:** Not currently available

## Character overview
Bestar is a Physical DPS hero specializing in Bleed damage and sustained damage against bleeding enemies. She summons black cats and spectral cats to attack opponents, applying Bleed effects that deal damage over time. Her passive increases damage against bleeding enemies, making her effective alongside other Bleed-inflicting heroes.

## Skills
- **Cat Bell (Auto Attack):** Throws a cat bell, dealing Physical damage to one enemy.
- **Black Cat Messenger (Active):** Summons a black cat to attack one enemy and apply Bleed, dealing Physical damage over time.
- **Sacred Flame Kitty (Ultimate):** Summons spectral cats to damage enemies within range and apply a longer-lasting Bleed effect.
- **Bloodstain Tracker (Passive):** Increases damage dealt to bleeding enemies. Star upgrades improve this bonus.
- **Tenacity (Support):** Fixed ATK, HP, and DEF bonus.

## Skill upgrade priority
1. **Bloodstain Tracker** — Improves damage against bleeding enemies, her core mechanic.
2. **Sacred Flame Kitty** — Improves area damage and Bleed application.
3. **Black Cat Messenger** — Improves single-target damage and Bleed application.
4. **Cat Bell** — Basic attack damage, lowest priority.

Tenacity is fixed and does not require regular upgrades.

## Gear priority
**UR Sword → UR Gauntlet.** Prioritize offensive gear to strengthen her Physical attacks and Bleed-related damage.

## Recommended team composition
- **Frontline:** Shadow (Ranger Tank), Louis (Ranger Tank)
- **Backline:** Bestar (Ranger DPS), Red Lady (Ranger DPS), Bell (Ranger Support)

This provides two tanks, support from Bell, and two damage dealers. Bestar and Red Lady can complement each other in Bleed-focused setups.

## Important mechanics
- **Bleed synergy:** Benefits from enemies bleeding, whether applied by Bestar or allies.
- **Area damage:** Sacred Flame Kitty damages and applies Bleed to multiple enemies.
- **Damage over time:** Bleed continues after the initial hit.
- **Positioning:** Keep Bestar protected in the backline.
