# Nicole — UR Warlock Support

**Faction:** Warlock
**Rarity:** UR
**Role:** Support
**Position:** Backline
**Damage Type:** Physical Damage

## Character Overview

Nicole is a Warlock support hero who combines healing, faction-wide offensive buffs, and area damage. She helps keep injured allies alive while strengthening the damage output of her Warlock teammates.

Her ultimate also deals increased damage against Burning enemies, making her especially compatible with Annie's Burn-focused abilities.

## Skills

### Ember Dust — Auto Attack
Deals Physical Damage to a single enemy while healing the ally with the lowest HP. Star upgrades strengthen both damage and healing.

### Heartwarming Flame — Active Skill
Heals the two allies with the lowest HP. Star upgrades significantly improve healing effectiveness.

### Inferno Array — Ultimate Skill
Summons a pillar of fire that damages all enemies. Deals additional damage to enemies affected by Burn.

### Flame Soul Power — Passive Skill
Increases the ATK of all allied Warlock heroes. Star upgrades also improve Warlock attack speed and strengthen the faction buff.

### Tenacity — Support Skill
Provides fixed ATK, HP, DEF, and skill cooldown speed bonuses. Does not require upgrading.

## Recommended Skill Upgrade Priority

1. **Flame Soul Power** — Faction-wide ATK benefits every Warlock ally, making this her most valuable long-term contribution.
2. **Heartwarming Flame** — Her primary healing ability keeps the team alive through sustained damage.
3. **Inferno Array** — Provides area damage and benefits from Burn effects applied by Annie.
4. **Ember Dust** — Useful supplemental healing and single-target damage, but less important than her other abilities.

**Tenacity** is fixed and does not require upgrading.

## Gear Priority

**UR Armor → UR Boots**

As a backline support, Nicole benefits from defensive gear that helps her survive and continue healing and buffing her teammates.
