# Arthur

**Faction:** Warrior
**Role:** Tank
**Position:** Frontline

## Character overview
Arthur is a durable frontline protector who specializes in absorbing damage and strengthening his team's defenses. His personal shielding, allied damage reduction, and Tank-focused passive make him particularly valuable in battles where survivability matters.

## Skills
- **Battle Shield — Auto Attack:** Strikes a single enemy with his shield, dealing physical damage.
- **Rock Solid — Active Skill:** Creates a protective shield based on his maximum HP, absorbing a portion of incoming damage.
- **Earthshattering — Ultimate Skill:** Damages nearby enemies and reduces physical damage received by allies within range.
- **Strong Will — Passive Skill:** Reduces all incoming damage received by allied Tank heroes.
- **Tenacity — Support Skill:** Increases ATK, HP, and DEF while accelerating skill cooldowns.

## Recommended skill upgrade priority
1. **Rock Solid** — Strengthens his personal shield and survivability.
2. **Strong Will** — Provides consistent damage reduction to allied Tanks.
3. **Earthshattering** — Protects nearby allies from physical damage while dealing area damage.
4. **Battle Shield** — Adds damage but contributes less to his main defensive role.

**Tenacity** is a fixed support skill and does not require upgrading.

## Gear priority
**Armor → Boots → other equipment.** Prioritize defensive attributes such as HP and DEF to strengthen Arthur's durability and shielding.

## Recommended team composition
- **Frontline:** Arthur (Tank), second Warrior Tank.
- **Backline:** Warrior DPS, Warrior DPS, Support.

Arthur pairs well with another frontline Tank, damage-focused Warrior heroes, and a backline Support who can sustain the team.

**Exclusive Weapon:** None.
**Awakening:** None.
