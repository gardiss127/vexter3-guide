# Celia — SSR Warrior Support

**Rarity:** SSR

## Character Overview
Celia is a Warrior support hero specializing in monster hunting and resource rewards. Unlike healing-focused supports, she contributes Physical damage through dagger attacks while improving her squad's effectiveness against monsters. Her defining skill, Ultimate Hunt, increases damage against monsters and the resources earned from defeating Resource and Elite Monsters.

## Skills
| Skill | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Flying Blade** | Auto Attack | Throws a dagger at a single enemy, dealing Physical damage. |
| **Deadly Lotus** | Active | Throws two daggers simultaneously, dealing Physical damage to two random targets. |
| **Ricochet Blade** | Ultimate | Throws a bouncing dagger that ricochets three times, damaging three random targets. |
| **Ultimate Hunt** | Passive | Increases damage dealt to monsters and boosts three types of resources obtained from defeating Resource and Elite Monsters. |
| **Tenacity** | Support | Provides a fixed bonus to ATK, HP, and DEF. |

## Skill Upgrade Priority
1. **Ultimate Hunt** — Defining support ability; improves monster damage and resource rewards.
2. **Ricochet Blade** — Strong multi-target Physical damage.
3. **Deadly Lotus** — Additional damage against multiple enemies.
4. **Flying Blade** — Basic single-target damage; lowest upgrade priority.

Tenacity is fixed and requires no normal skill upgrades.

## Gear Priority
**UR Armor → UR Boots.** As a support hero, Celia benefits from survivability, helping her remain active throughout monster encounters.

## Recommended Team Composition
- **Frontline:** Arthur + Daskal
- **Backline:** Marlena + Zoya + Celia

Example Warrior lineup for PvE and resource farming. Celia replaces a more conventional support when her monster-damage and resource bonuses are desirable.
