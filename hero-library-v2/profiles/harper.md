## Hero Overview
Harper is a defensive and offensive support hero who helps his team survive incoming damage while increasing its overall attack power. His Warrior-specific buff makes him particularly effective in full Warrior squads.

## Skills

**Bubble — Auto Attack**

Plays a flute to create bubbles that deal Energy Damage to a single enemy. Star upgrades improve its damage.

**Vitality Bubble — Active Skill**

Creates a protective bubble that reduces incoming damage for the ally with the lowest HP. At its third skill star, the ability can protect an additional ally.

**War Flute Sound — Ultimate Skill**

Plays a war flute to increase the ATK of all allied heroes temporarily. Skill-star upgrades add an Energy Damage bonus for allies, strengthen the ATK increase, and extend its duration.

**Warrior's Anthem — Passive Skill**

Increases the ATK of all allied Warrior heroes throughout battle. Skill-star upgrades further strengthen this faction-specific bonus.

**Tenacity — Support Skill**

Provides bonuses to ATK, HP, DEF, and skill cooldown speed. This skill is fixed at level 1.

## Recommended Skill Upgrade Priority

1. **War Flute Sound** — Teamwide ATK increase and additional Energy Damage support.
2. **Warrior's Anthem** — Consistent ATK boost for all Warrior teammates.
3. **Vitality Bubble** — Protects vulnerable allies from incoming damage.
4. **Bubble** — Personal damage is less important than his support abilities.

If your squad needs more protection, prioritize Vitality Bubble earlier.

## Recommended Gear

**Armor → Boots**

Prioritize UR defensive equipment to improve Harper's survivability. Keeping him alive allows him to continue providing team buffs and protection.

## Recommended Squad Composition

**Frontline:** Arthur — Tank; Daskal — Tank.

**Backline:** Marlena — DPS; Zoya — DPS; Harper — Support.

**Squad synergy:** Arthur and Daskal provide frontline protection, Marlena and Zoya supply damage, and Harper supports the entire team with ATK buffs and damage reduction.

His Warrior's Anthem benefits all five heroes, making this a natural faction-focused composition.
