# Kesso — SSR Warrior DPS

**Rarity:** SSR
**Faction:** Warrior
**Role:** DPS
**Position:** Backline
**Damage Type:** Physical
**Exclusive Weapon:** None
**Awakening:** Not currently available

## Character overview
Kesso is a physical damage dealer who specializes in multi-target spear attacks and summoning a war eagle named Rex to assist in battle. His abilities combine direct damage, an additional summoned attacker, and a temporary ATK boost when his health becomes critically low. He is best positioned behind two frontline tanks.

## Skills
- **Spear Throw (Auto Attack):** Throws a spear at a single enemy, dealing Physical damage. Star upgrades increase its damage.
- **Hunting Skills (Active):** Quickly throws two spears at randomly selected enemies, dealing Physical damage to each. A later star upgrade adds another split shot.
- **Summon Warhawk (Ultimate):** Summons Rex, a war eagle that fights alongside Kesso for a limited duration. Rex inherits combat attributes from Kesso, and star upgrades improve Rex's inherited ATK.
- **Wild Survival (Passive):** When Kesso's HP falls below a critical threshold for the first time, he temporarily gains increased ATK. Star upgrades improve the bonus and its duration.
- **Tenacity (Support):** Provides a fixed bonus to ATK, HP, and DEF.

## Skill upgrade priority
1. **Summon Warhawk** — Prioritize Rex's summoned damage contribution.
2. **Hunting Skills** — Strengthens his multi-target spear attacks.
3. **Wild Survival** — Improves his conditional ATK boost.
4. **Spear Throw** — Basic attack damage; lowest priority.

Tenacity is fixed and does not require regular upgrades.

## Gear priority
**UR Sword → UR Gauntlet.** Prioritize offensive equipment to strengthen Kesso's Physical damage and overall damage output.

## Recommended team composition
- **Frontline:** Arthur + Daskal (Warrior Tanks)
- **Backline:** Kesso + another Warrior DPS + Harper (Warrior Support)

This composition protects Kesso while allowing him to contribute sustained damage through his spear attacks and Rex.

## Important mechanics
- Rex is a summoned combat companion, not a permanent additional hero.
- Hunting Skills initially throws two spears, with an additional split shot unlocked through star progression.
- Wild Survival activates only when Kesso's HP falls below its threshold for the first time in combat.
