## Character Overview
Sivir is a Physical DPS hero who combines single-target attacks with multi-target damage. Her passive skill, Hunter Bloodline, increases her ATK during combat, strengthening her offensive abilities. She is best suited to a Warrior squad where frontline tanks protect her while she deals damage from the backline.

## Skills
- **Hunt (Auto Attack):** Throws a broken spear at a single enemy, dealing Physical damage.
- **Shield Strike (Active):** Throws a shield at one enemy, dealing Physical damage.
- **Wild Pursuit (Ultimate):** Throws two broken spears at randomly selected enemies, dealing Physical damage. Star upgrades improve damage and can increase the number of targets.
- **Hunter Bloodline (Passive):** Increases Sivir's ATK during combat, improving her overall damage output.
- **Tenacity (Support):** Provides a fixed bonus to ATK, HP, and DEF. Unlocks when the hero reaches the required level and star rank.

## Skill Upgrade Priority
1. **Hunter Bloodline** — Strengthens all of Sivir's attacks through its ATK bonus.
2. **Wild Pursuit** — Multi-target ultimate damage and additional target potential through star upgrades.
3. **Shield Strike** — Single-target active damage.
4. **Hunt** — Basic attack damage; lowest upgrade priority.

Tenacity is a fixed support skill and does not require normal skill upgrades.

## Gear Priority
**UR Sword → UR Gauntlet.** Prioritize offensive gear to increase Physical damage output.
