## Character Overview

Joker is a Physical Damage dealer who specializes in targeting weakened enemies, delivering powerful multi-target attacks, and increasing his critical hit rate. His abilities make him effective at finishing off low-HP opponents. At higher star levels, Hearthunt Red Card can also inflict Burn, creating synergy with Warlock allies such as Annie and Nicole.

## Skills

- **Deal Cards (Auto Attack):** Throws a card at the enemy with the lowest HP, dealing Physical Damage. Star upgrades increase damage.
- **Hearthunt Red Card (Active Skill):** Throws a heart card at two random enemies, dealing Physical Damage. At higher stars, the attack also inflicts Burn.
- **Joker Ace (Ultimate Skill):** Throws two joker cards at the two enemies with the lowest HP, dealing heavy Physical Damage. Higher-star upgrades increase damage and add another target.
- **Frenzied Emotion (Passive Skill):** Increases Joker's critical hit rate, improving his overall damage potential.
- **Tenacity (Support Skill):** Provides fixed ATK, HP, DEF, and skill cooldown speed bonuses. Does not require upgrading.

## Skill Upgrade Priority

1. **Joker Ace** — Highest priority for powerful finishing damage against weakened enemies.
2. **Frenzied Emotion** — Improves critical hit consistency across Joker's attacks.
3. **Hearthunt Red Card** — Provides additional multi-target damage and eventually Burn application.
4. **Deal Cards** — Useful single-target damage, but lower priority than his stronger abilities.

Tenacity is fixed and does not need upgrading.
