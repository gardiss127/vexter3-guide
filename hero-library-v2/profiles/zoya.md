# Zoya

**Faction:** Warrior
**Role:** DPS
**Position:** Backline

## Character overview
Zoya is a backline Warrior damage dealer who uses Energy attacks, area damage, increased Energy damage taken by enemies, and critical strikes to pressure opponents.

## Skills
- **Dagger — Auto Attack:** Deals Energy damage to a single target.
- **Mountain God Wrath — Active Skill:** Unleashes a whirlwind that damages enemies in an area and increases the Energy damage they take.
- **Forest Hunter — Ultimate Skill:** Throws multiple daggers at random enemies, dealing Energy damage. Star upgrades add daggers and improve damage.
- **Lady of the Forest — Passive Skill:** Increases Critical Hit Rate.
- **Tenacity — Fixed Support Skill:** Provides ATK, HP, DEF and skill cooldown speed bonuses; not upgraded.

## Recommended skill upgrade priority
1. **Forest Hunter** — Main multi-hit offensive ability.
2. **Mountain God Wrath** — Area damage and Energy vulnerability.
3. **Lady of the Forest** — Critical strike improvement.
4. **Dagger** — Regular single-target attack.

**Tenacity** is fixed and does not require upgrading.

## Gear priority
**UR Sword → UR Gauntlet.** Prioritize damage output.

## Recommended team composition
- **Frontline:** Arthur (Tank) + Daskal (Tank)
- **Backline:** Marlena (DPS) + Zoya (DPS) + Harper (Support)

**Exclusive Weapon:** None.
**Awakening:** Not currently available.
