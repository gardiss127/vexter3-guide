## Character overview
Claire is a backline Warrior DPS who specializes in powerful Physical Damage attacks, rapid multi-hit strikes, and area damage. Her abilities are particularly effective against monsters, and she increases the damage dealt by the team's strongest Warrior attackers.

## Skills
- **Adv. Holy Banner — Auto Attack:** Swings her battle flag to deal Physical Damage to a single enemy. Star upgrades increase the attack's damage.
- **Adv. Rain of Arrows — Active Skill:** Unleashes five rapid arrow attacks against a single target, dealing repeated Physical Damage. Star upgrades significantly increase damage.
- **Adv. Celestial Judgment — Ultimate Skill:** Summons a giant holy sword that strikes enemies within range, dealing heavy area Physical Damage. Star upgrades increase damage against monsters and strengthen the attack.
- **Adv. Call of the Brave — Passive Skill:** Increases damage dealt to monsters by the three Warriors with the highest ATK in the squad. Star upgrades strengthen this bonus.
- **Adv. Tenacity — Fixed Support Skill:** Provides ATK, HP, DEF, and skill cooldown speed bonuses. Fixed at level 1 and does not require upgrading.

## Recommended skill upgrade priority
1. **Adv. Celestial Judgment** — Strongest area-damage ability, with additional value against monsters.
2. **Adv. Call of the Brave** — Increases monster damage for the squad's three strongest Warrior attackers; especially valuable in PvE.
3. **Adv. Rain of Arrows** — Strong single-target damage through repeated attacks.
4. **Adv. Holy Banner** — Basic attack damage; lower priority than her other abilities.

**Adv. Tenacity** is fixed and does not require upgrading.

## Gear priority
**UR Sword → UR Gauntlet.** Prioritize offensive equipment to maximize Physical Damage output.

## Recommended team composition
- **Frontline:** Arthur (Tank) + Daskal (Tank)
- **Backline:** Marlena (DPS) + Claire (DPS) + Harper (Support)

**Squad synergy:** Arthur and Daskal protect the frontline, Marlena and Claire provide damage, and Harper supplies offensive buffs and protection. Claire's monster-damage passive makes this composition especially attractive for PvE encounters. Claire can also be considered as an alternative to Zoya in monster-focused Warrior squads.
