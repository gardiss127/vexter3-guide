## Character Overview
Hastar is a frontline Warlock tank who combines Energy damage, crowd control, and personal survivability. His tentacle-based abilities allow him to interrupt enemies, damage multiple targets, and protect himself when his health becomes critically low.

His primary strengths are stunning enemies and surviving dangerous encounters, making him a defensive option for Warlock squads.

## Skills
- **Lash (Auto Attack):** Strikes a single enemy with tentacles, dealing Energy damage.
- **Undercurrent (Active):** Summons tentacles from the ground to knock a single enemy into the air, dealing Energy damage and stunning the target briefly.
- **Wrath of Old Gods (Ultimate):** Slams a massive tentacle into the ground, dealing substantial Energy damage to enemies in front of Hastar.
- **Scorn Guard (Passive):** When Hastar's HP first falls below a critical threshold, he gains a temporary shield that absorbs part of incoming damage. Star upgrades strengthen the shield and extend its duration.
- **Tenacity (Support):** Provides a fixed bonus to ATK, HP, and DEF.

## Skill Upgrade Priority
1. **Scorn Guard** — Improves emergency survivability, his most important tanking ability.
2. **Undercurrent** — Improves his crowd-control attack. The stun helps interrupt enemy actions.
3. **Wrath of Old Gods** — Strengthens damage against enemies positioned in front of him.
4. **Lash** — Basic single-target damage; lowest priority for a defensive build.

Tenacity is a fixed support skill and does not require regular upgrades.

## Gear Priority
**UR Armor → UR Boots.** Focus on survivability. Armor and boots help Hastar remain alive long enough to activate his defensive shield and continue controlling enemies.

## Recommended Team Composition
- **Frontline:** Hastar (Tank), Warlock Tank (second frontline)
- **Backline:** Warlock DPS, Warlock DPS, Warlock Support

Hastar fits a standard two-tank, two-DPS, one-support Warlock formation. Specific teammates can be finalized as we verify the remaining Warlock heroes.
