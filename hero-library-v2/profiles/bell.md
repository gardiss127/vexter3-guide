# Bell

**Faction:** Ranger
**Role:** Support / Healer / ATK buffer
**Position:** Backline

## Character overview
Backline Ranger support who combines healing with offensive buffs for the team's strongest damage dealers.

## Skills
- **Musical Note — Auto Attack:** Physical single-target attack.
- **Healing Sound — Active Skill:** Heals the ally with the lowest HP.
- **Inspiration — Ultimate Skill:** Temporarily increases ATK of the two highest-ATK allies; at 10-star hero upgrade, adds a third target.
- **Battle Anthem — Passive Skill:** Increases ATK of allied Rangers.
- **Tenacity — Fixed Support Skill:** Fixed stat and cooldown bonuses; not upgraded.

## Recommended skill upgrade priority
1. **Inspiration** — Main offensive buff.
2. **Healing Sound** — Team sustain.
3. **Battle Anthem** — Ranger-wide offensive bonus.
4. **Musical Note** — Basic attack.

## Gear priority
**UR Armor → UR Boots** for survivability.

## Recommended team composition
**Frontline:** Shadow + Louis.
**Backline:** Cynthia + Red Lady + Bell.

**Exclusive Weapon:** None.
**Awakening:** Not currently available.
